The anterior/posterior polygon coordinates were converted from
`src/assets/index.ts` to JSON and are rendered natively with SwiftUI Paths.
No React runtime is included. This is reference artwork, not patient anatomy
or a visualization of measured regional body composition.

The Body training view can color regions by logged hard sets for the selected
week. This represents training volume only, never regional body fat, muscle
measurements or a body scan. Hover, tap or choose a region for sets and exercises.
