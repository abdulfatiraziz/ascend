the side/back of a head. Reduce Motion disables pointer tilt and stage animation.

from 50% until the goal is reached, Ascendant at 100%. The cycle goal already
accounts for the configured consistency target (75% by default). Time alone does
not earn a stage. Appearance overrides and previews do not change habit scores.
